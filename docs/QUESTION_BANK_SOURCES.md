# Question-bank reference leads and scope

All question wording, fictional examples, numeric exercises, answer sets, and explanations were generated for this project with AI assistance and are contributed under MIT. Reference materials were consulted for facts, topic planning, and grade-band guidance; no reference images or question-bank text are bundled. Links do not incorporate a source's license into the project or imply source endorsement.

The references below were consulted during authoring on 2026-11-21/22. They are **review leads**, not a completed item-by-item factual or pedagogical review. Each JSON item links its relevant lead. The owner should replace broad leads with more specific citations during actual review and verify all statements, including paraphrases and distractors. Generated scenes, route data, prices, measurements, and tiny stories are fictional; their answers follow the stated data.
