# Implementation decisions

The README, MVP_REQUIREMENTS, and OPEN_DECISIONS remain the source of truth. This document records implementation choices and the calibration decision explicitly approved during implementation.

- Native SwiftUI; iOS/iPadOS 17+, iPhone and iPad; portrait and landscape, including upside-down on iPad. A centered, scrolling column adapts to large text and window size. Light appearance uses the specified palette; native button labels pair feedback with words/icons. No in-app speech feature.
- Dependency-free Swift package for deterministic learning and timing; app state in protected, atomic JSON in Application Support. No cloud, telemetry, ads, accounts, or runtime generated content. Exclude progress from backups.
- School: math, reading, science. General knowledge: world, nature, everyday. Alternate school/general questions; balance subjects within each group. Prefer unseen question IDs; never repeat an ID within a round. Choose the nearest available difficulty that fits the selected grade's reading ceiling. A thin bank can prevent an exact-level match; this must be fixed through reviewed content, not by inventing a new review status.
- Six content levels map to K–5. One wrong/unsure response lowers only that subject one level and clears its streak. Ten correct responses promote only that subject one level and clear its streak. Clamp at K and 5. Streaks persist in app state, independent of round/session boundaries. Grade changes adjust reading and future session caps while preserving subject learning.
- **Approved calibration rule:** extend after 10 only if a subject changed difficulty in the round; finish once all changed subjects have two subsequent answers at their new level. A later change resets that subject's counter. Finish in pairs, so K–2 extensions end by 14 (within the 15 cap); grades 3–5 by 20. Session exhaustion always wins, including during feedback.
- Correct answers also show an explanation and wait for Next. Recap uses the last two explanations, with no score. A partial round preserves its answers for reports. Unanswered questions are not accuracy attempts; unsure is an attempted, non-correct response. Mandatory break discards a pending answer; short interruption resumes it.
- One review opportunity per six question positions, for a previously missed/unsure item from another session, at least 30 minutes later. Nearest difficulty, group balance, reading ceiling, and within-round uniqueness still apply. Fresh questions otherwise rank first. Sparse content can still repeat between rounds.
- All foreground child screens, including home, recap, and explanations, count. Parent screens and inactive/background time pause counting. Checkpoint each second and on lifecycle changes. Continuous monotonic boot time (including device sleep) measures play and absence; both wall and monotonic absence must reach 30 minutes on the same boot. Thirty uninterrupted minutes outside play refreshes allowance. The original session grade fixes its cap until the next session.
- If killed while marked foreground, charge elapsed time since the last checkpoint conservatively on relaunch. If uptime moved backward, charge the remaining allowance and restart the break. Clock rollback restarts the away interval; forward wall time alone cannot shorten it. This prioritizes enforcing limits over recovering uncertain seconds. A mismatch of more than five seconds between wall elapsed time and uptime elapsed time also restarts the away interval conservatively. This detects usual restarts even when the new uptime exceeds the previous value. This is not tamper-proof against deliberate clock manipulation on a compromised device.
- Parent PIN: six ASCII digits, confirmation at setup, device-only Keychain PBKDF2 credential, five guesses then five-minute cooldown. No recovery bypass; family owner must reset data using Xcode/device management if forgotten. Parent view relocks on dismissal and any inactive phase. Show a privacy cover when inactive.
- Parent overview and difficulty trend summarize the retained 90-day detail; total foreground minutes is lifetime. No export feature. No automatic online backups. Data deletion beyond retention requires the family owner to remove the app data; there is no child-facing delete/reset control.
